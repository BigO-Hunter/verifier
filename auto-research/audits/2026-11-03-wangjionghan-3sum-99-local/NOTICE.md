This audit replays Wang-jiong-han's pinned Lean formalization of the 3SUM
exponent `384055881637/200000000000` (bigospeedrun/verifier issue #99). The
submission repository `Wang-jiong-han/3sum-weighted-cache` at commit
`e1ae71db2231f172c6541820e2ce0a54aa14fc64` and the verifier repository are both
Apache-2.0. This package contains only audit code, logs, and digests; it copies
no part of the source proof.

The submission's own `PROVENANCE.md` attributes the parameter refinement and
the formal cache-model optimization certificate to Jionghan Wang (University of
Science and Technology of China), records that the complete solver builds on
the verified source project `proof-factory/3sum-bound` at commit `8af82141` and
its contributors, including c0ldzy17, JoshuaWharton and HrishikSaiBojnal, and
preserves the Anthropic, PBC copyright and Apache-2.0 notices on the original
RAM benchmark semantics together with the inherited record of the algorithmic
work of Josh Alman and Virginia Vassilevska Williams. Those credits and the
upstream LICENSE and NOTICE are retained in the submission repository; this
audit adds nothing to and claims nothing about them.
