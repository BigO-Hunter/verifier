This audit replays Wang-jiong-han's pinned Lean formalization of the 3SUM
exponent `96013971/50000000` (bigospeedrun/verifier issue #92). The submission
repository `Wang-jiong-han/3sum-weighted-cache` at commit
`ac885d10bdbf723c3e0549198f9345bedf2a4131` and the verifier repository are both
Apache-2.0. This package contains only audit code, logs, and digests; it copies
no part of the source proof.

The submission's own header credits `proof-factory/3sum-bound`, the ternary
construction of c0ldzy17 and contributors, Joshua Wharton's numerical
certificates, Hrishik Sai Bojnal's parameter refinements, and Anthropic's
word-RAM specification and compiler. Those credits and the upstream LICENSE and
NOTICE are retained in the submission repository; this audit adds nothing to
and claims nothing about them.
